About

I'm midnightblue. 27, from Tunisia, with a CS degree I mostly went through the motions of.

I'm learning to build AI agents and tools, and I'm writing down the whole thing as it happens. The wins, the "no clue" moments, the bugs that turn out to be somewhere else entirely.

I'd call myself creatively practical. I don't just want to imagine things. I want to make useful things, my way.

The goal is a remote job at a company building AI agents. This blog is the road there.
